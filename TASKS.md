- [ ] **The fastai AND huggingface backends cannot use DataLoader WORKER PROCESSES** @medium @bug — found by `examples/sweep/run_sweep.sh` on 2026-08-02, which is what a sweep is for: three of thirteen cells died in ~11-29s with `RuntimeError: DataLoader worker (pid(s) …) exited unexpectedly` and no child traceback, while lightning / torch / keras / tensorflow ran the SAME config (`num_workers: 8`) fine. Next step is one fit with a single worker under `faulthandler` to read what the child actually dies of, rather than guessing at macOS fork-safety. Until then both backends need `num_workers: 0`.
