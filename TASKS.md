- [ ] **Migrate the remaining FIVE backends onto the trainer bases** @medium @refactor @cross-project — the 2026-08-06 consolidation landed the base architecture (matrainer `TrainerRunnable` → `TorchTrainerRunnable` → `LightningRunnable`; sonair `classification_base.py` + `classification_torch.py`) and moved `LightningClassifier` onto it; 2026-08-07 added `matrainer.torch.TorchLoopRunnable` + `matrainer.fastai.FastaiRunnable` and moved `TorchClassifier` and `FastaiClassifier` too. When a family lands here, its backends implement the hooks from `classification_base`/`classification_torch` instead of private copies, and its fleet-test patterns get the shared-base exemption `test_tracking_parity.py` already carries for lightning and the torch loop.
- [ ] **`matrainer.fastai` tracking callback** @medium @cross-project — every other backend forwards PER-EPOCH metrics to matrainer trackers (`matrainer.lightning.TrackingCallback`, `matrainer.keras.keras_tracking_callback`); the fastai backend currently writes only the end-of-run artifacts (`save_class_names` + `write_metrics_sidecar`); its `callbacks` list is internal and holds nothing that reports per-epoch values. The missing piece is a `fastai.callback.core.Callback` that forwards `learn.recorder` values, and it belongs in **matrainer**, not sonair (the "generic primitives live upstream" mandate) — so it is a matrainer task with a one-line sonair follow-up in `_callbacks()`.
